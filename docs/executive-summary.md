# NEXUS Trade Executive Summary

## Problem

Forex traders struggle to read market structure, find strong setups, and know whether a strategy actually works before risking money on it.

## Intended Users

Anyone interested in forex trading who wants clearer, data-backed trade ideas.

## General Purpose

NEXUS helps traders make better decisions by analyzing the market, picking trade setups, backtesting them on past data, and highlighting the top-performing currency pairs.

## Initial Scope

NEXUS analyzes, backtests, and suggests trades on an OANDA practice (demo) account only; it will not trade real money, and it will never suggest a trade with a reward-to-risk ratio below 2.
